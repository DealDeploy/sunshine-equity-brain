# Sunshine Equity — AI brain

This repo is the AI brain for **Sunshine Equity Partners**, a fictional
growth-equity firm used for product demos.

All names, companies, people, and figures are invented. Do not treat them as
live investments or as data about any real firm.

## Where knowledge lives

| Path | Use for |
|------|---------|
| [firm/](./firm/) | Curated firm brain — start at [firm/overview.md](./firm/overview.md) |
| [firm/GLOSSARY.md](./firm/GLOSSARY.md) | Firm language (sourcing, sprint, MOIC, …) |
| [firm/team/](./firm/team/) | People and roles — [firm/team/index.md](./firm/team/index.md) |
| [firm/playbooks/](./firm/playbooks/) | How to produce firm deliverables (e.g. IC memo) |
| [firm/research/](./firm/research/) | Non-canonical investigations |
| [neutron/](./neutron/) | Published wiki evidence |

**Deliverables:** When drafting firm work product (IC memo, etc.), open the matching file under `firm/playbooks/` and follow it. Use `neutron/` for examples and source facts.

**Terms:** Prefer [firm/GLOSSARY.md](./firm/GLOSSARY.md) over improvised PE jargon.
