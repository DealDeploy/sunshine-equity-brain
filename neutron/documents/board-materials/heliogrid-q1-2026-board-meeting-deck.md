**Document type:** Board deck for HelioGrid (commercial solar fleet-operations software) Q1 2026 board meeting. Chair: Maya Ellison.

**Agenda:** CEO update; financials vs. plan; fleet / product KPIs; enterprise pipeline; engineering allocation; CRO 90-day plan; closed session.

**CEO update (Elena Varga):** Q1 revenue $4.6M vs. $4.4M plan. Two 2025 renewal risks (Northline Solar, Red Mesa C&I) closed. CRO **Dana Ruiz** started February 17. Services mix 11% of revenue (board asked to keep it under 12%). Denver office lease renewed through 2028.

**Financials (Q1 2026):** ARR $18.1M exiting March (vs. $16.4M YE 2025). Gross margin 82.4%. OpEx $4.1M. Adjusted EBITDA ($0.18M) vs. plan ($0.31M). Cash $14.2M, no debt. Headcount 97.

**Fleet KPIs:** Sites under management 6,410 (+14% YoY). Inverter models ingested: 41. Mean time to dispatch a work order: 3.4 hours (was 5.1 a year ago). Gridroom uptime 99.92%.

**Product:** Q1 commits — multi-OEM event bus, owner PDF pack v2, mobile work-order offline mode. Q2 bets: “fleet copilot” anomaly summaries (labeled beta; not in the revenue plan).

**Enterprise:** Pipeline $4.9M weighted. Two IPP evaluations in late stage (Harborlight Power expansion; new logo Windrow Energy). Win rate last four quarters 28%.

**Engineering allocation:** RUN 34% / GROW 48% / TRANSFORM 18%. Hiring plan: +6 engineers in 2026, biased to data platform.

**Housekeeping:** 2026 in-person boards May 12 (Denver), August 20 (Denver), November 10 (virtual). Monthly calls the third Thursday.
