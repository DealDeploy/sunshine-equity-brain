**Document**: Internal Sunshine sprint output, **4 November 2025**. Not an IC thesis. Authors Priya Nandakumar and Theo Marlowe.

**Scope:** Software and marketplace layers around industrial process heat below ~200°C in North America. Hardware OEMs are context, not targets.

**Working conclusions:**
- Specification, incentive paperwork, and M&V are still spreadsheet-heavy. That is the Amberline Spec motion.
- Runtime optimization (setpoints, load shifting) is a different buyer and budget. That is the Kilnwise motion. Do not merge TAM.
- OEM channel interviews (n=6, anonymized) split 3 “will partner,” 2 “will build a thin portal,” 1 “already bundling.” Treat OEM partnership as a scenario, not a base case.
- Utility incentive rules are state-specific; any national TAM that assumes one program is wrong.

**Implication for pipeline:** Amberline confirmatory (then in flight) should underwrite Spec as the recurring core. Marketplace GMV is upside. Kilnwise remains a separate platform; a webinar is not a channel.

**Not in this note:** any HelioGrid or Tidalspan analysis.
