**Document**: Capital call letter from Sunshine Equity Partners GP II to Fund II LPs, dated **June 18, 2024**.

**Call**: **$32.0mm** net to fund a new preferred equity investment in HelioGrid, Inc., plus a de minimis expense true-up. Wire instructions in the appendix (demo text only; no real banking details).

**Deal snapshot** (as in the IC memo):
- $32.0mm preferred, 1.0x liquidation preference, 8% accruing dividend.
- $68.0mm pre-money EV; Sunshine Fund II ~38% fully diluted.
- $12.0mm primary / $20.0mm secondary.
- Board: two Sunshine seats (Maya Ellison, Jordan Voss).

**Thesis (LP-facing, abbreviated)**:
- HelioGrid is fleet-operations software for commercial solar. Independent power producers still run multi-OEM fleets on spreadsheets and vendor portals.
- 2023A ARR $6.8mm growing to $11.2mm 2024E; 93% GRR / 118% NRR on the core product.
- Entry ~6.1x ARR. Base-case underwriting 3.1x MOIC / 22% IRR at a 2028 exit.

**Diligence**: accounting (Northglass), technology (Bittern), legal (Hale & Rowan), 12 references. No material open items at call date.

**Use of proceeds (company)**: enterprise sales hiring, customer-success build, and product (multi-OEM ingestion). Secondary is liquidity for early holders; CEO remains and rolled the majority of her stake.
