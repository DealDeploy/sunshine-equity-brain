## Overview
- **Document**: Sunshine Equity Partners internal investment memo on **HelioGrid, Inc.**, dated **June 11, 2024** (author Priya Nandakumar; lead partner Maya Ellison).
- HelioGrid (founded 2016, Denver) sells fleet-operations software for commercial and industrial solar: work-order O&M, inverter-level performance, and owner reporting via **Gridroom**.
- 94 logos; ~58% of ARR from independent power producers and yieldcos (Northline Solar, Solstice Cooperative, Harborlight Power); rest from C&I owner-operators and a small EPC channel.

## Financials (Company-reported, $mm)
- ARR: $4.1 (2022A) → $6.8 (2023A) → $11.2 (2024E at signing); run-rate ARR ~$10.5mm at deal time vs $11.2mm 2024E — note the 2024E figure includes $0.7mm of unsigned expansion that diligence haircut to $0.4mm.
- Total GAAP Revenue: $3.8 (2022A) → $6.1 (2023A) → $10.4E (2024E).
- EBITDA: ($2.1) 2022A, ($1.4) 2023A, ($0.6) 2024E — approaching breakeven; forecast to turn EBITDA-positive in 2026E ($1.8mm) per detailed model, reaching $8.4mm / 29% margin by 2028E (Base case).
- Gross margin: ~88% on software, ~81% blended (2023A) after field-services pass-through.
- Retention (core Gridroom): 93% gross, 118% net (2023); overall GRR ~90% after a 2022 module that was sunset.

## Deal Terms
- Sunshine Fund II investing **$32.0mm** in preferred equity with **1.0x liquidation preference** and **8% accruing dividend**.
- Pre-money enterprise value: **$68,000,000** (~6.1x run-rate ARR of $11.2mm), fully diluted.
- Sunshine to own **38%** of company post-close; remaining 62% held by founders, employees, and a seed vehicle (Cinder Trail Capital).
- Board: Sunshine to hold 2 of 5 seats — Maya Ellison and Jordan Voss — plus an independent chair to be recruited.
- Use of proceeds: $12.0mm primary (enterprise sales, product, customer success); $20.0mm secondary (liquidity to early holders and a partial founder sale, founder remains CEO).

## Management
- **Elena Varga** – Co-founder / CEO (prior plant-ops software at a mid-size inverter OEM).
- **Tomas Quayle** – CFO (joined 2023; prior controller at a publicly listed yieldco).
- **Sable Okoye** – VP Product.
- **Chris Pell** – VP Sales (built the IPP motion in 2022–23).

## Diligence
- 12 expert / reference calls, including two former heads of asset management at independent power producers, one O&M contractor CEO, and a former product lead at a monitoring incumbent.
- Customer references: Northline Solar, Solstice Cooperative, Red Mesa C&I, Two Rivers Food Co-op (rooftop fleet).
- Third-party diligence: Northglass Advisory (accounting), Bittern Technical (technology), Hale & Rowan (legal), Pinion Benefits (insurance).

## Investment Thesis Highlights
- Commercial solar fleets are still run on a patchwork of OEM portals and spreadsheets; Gridroom’s multi-OEM ingestion is the switching-cost story.
- Pricing: $4–7k ACV for C&I sites, $40–90k ACV for IPP portfolios; ~65% of new ARR via a four-person enterprise team.
- Logo growth: 31 → 94 since 2021; ARR ~65% CAGR 2022–2024E.
- NRR 118% driven by site adds inside existing IPP contracts, not price.
- Channel: two regional O&M contractors resell Gridroom; not yet a meaningful mix (~8% of 2023 bookings).

## Returns Analysis (Scenario Table, $mm unless noted)
- **Base case**: 3.1x MOIC, 22% IRR; 2028E revenue $29.1mm; 2028 EBITDA $8.4mm (29% margin); exit multiple 12.0x EBITDA; assumes 92% GRR, 3% annual price, 22 new logos/yr after 2025.
- **Growth case**: 4.6x MOIC, 31% IRR; 2028E revenue $36.8mm; EBITDA $12.1mm; exit 14.0x; 95% GRR, +8 logos/yr vs base.
- **Downside case**: 1.3x MOIC, 5% IRR; 2028E revenue $18.4mm; EBITDA $2.9mm; exit 9.0x; 86% GRR, no price, logo adds stall after 2025.

## Risks and Mitigants
- **OEM portal competition**: inverter vendors ship free monitoring; mitigant is multi-OEM fleet reporting and work orders, which single-OEM portals do not do.
- **Services mix**: field-tech marketplace is 12% of revenue and lower margin; memo treats it as optional, not in base-case exit multiple.
- **Customer concentration**: top 5 logos = 41% of ARR; two are in renewal windows in 2025.
- **Key-person**: CEO is the IPP relationship; board will fund a CRO search in year one.
- **Valuation**: 6.1x ARR is a premium vs. Sunshine’s Fund I entries; diligence comps clustered 5–9x for similarly scaled vertical SaaS.

## Structure/Governance Post-Close
- Sunshine preferred = 38% FD; protective provisions on budget, M&A, and follow-on.
- Majority common remains with management and Cinder Trail; Sunshine does **not** have unilateral control.
