## Overview
- **Document**: Sunshine Equity Partners internal investment memo on **Tidalspan Ltd.**, dated **September 22, 2023** (author Reese Calder; lead partner Jordan Voss).
- Tidalspan (founded 2018, Halifax) sells berth, laydown, and vessel-window software to ports and EPCs staging offshore-wind components. Product line **Quayline**.
- 41 logos across Canada, the UK, and the US East Coast; mix of port authorities (Harborlight Ports Authority, Greybank Terminals) and EPCs.

## Financials (Company-reported, $mm)
- ARR: $2.4 (2021A) → $3.9 (2022A) → $5.7 (2023E); GAAP revenue similar given high subscription mix.
- EBITDA: ($1.8) 2021A, ($1.1) 2022A, ($0.4) 2023E.
- Gross margin ~84% (2022A). GRR 91%, NRR 112% (2022).
- Geographic mix: 48% Canada, 31% UK, 21% US at signing.

## Deal Terms
- Sunshine Fund II investing **$18.0mm** preferred, **1.0x** non-participating, **6%** accruing dividend.
- Pre-money EV **$42.0mm** (~7.4x 2023E ARR).
- Sunshine **28%** FD; two of five board seats (Jordan Voss plus an independent to be named).
- $10.0mm primary / $8.0mm secondary.

## Management
- **Harun Bekele** – Founder / CEO (ex-port operations, Halifax).
- **Moira Glen** – COO.
- **Edwin Park** – Head of Product.

## Investment Thesis Highlights
- Offshore-wind staging is a capacity-constrained, project-based workflow; software that books laydown and vessel windows has a natural seat at the port.
- Switching cost is operational, not contractual: once a terminal’s night shift runs Quayline, paper booking does not return.
- US East Coast build-out is the growth case; UK is the reference market.

## Returns
- **Base**: 2.8x MOIC, 19% IRR, 2028 exit at 11x EBITDA.
- **Growth**: 4.1x / 28% (US ports land).
- **Downside**: 1.2x / 4% (project delays, logo freeze).

## Risks
- **Policy / project timing**: offshore-wind FIDs slip; mitigant is a port-authority installed base that also serves breakbulk and heavy lift.
- **Geographic concentration**: Canada still ~half of ARR.
- **Implementation**: average time-to-value 5 months; diligence flagged two stalled UK rollouts.
