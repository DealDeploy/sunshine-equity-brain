# Company {#company}

HelioGrid builds **Gridroom**, fleet-operations software for commercial and industrial solar.

Founded 2016, Denver. CEO Elena Varga (co-founder). 94 logos at signing.

Customers include independent power producers and yieldcos (Northline Solar, Solstice Cooperative, Harborlight Power) and C&I owner-operators (Red Mesa C&I, Two Rivers Food Co-op).

# Thesis {#thesis}

1. Multi-OEM commercial fleets still run on vendor portals and spreadsheets.
2. Gridroom's ingestion and work orders are the switching cost.
3. NRR is site-adds inside existing IPP contracts, not price.
4. Entry at 6.1x ARR is inside the 5–9x vertical-SaaS band we diligenced.
5. Structure (preferred, two seats, 38%) matches a growth recap where we do not need unilateral control.
