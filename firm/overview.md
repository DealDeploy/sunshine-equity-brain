# Sunshine Equity Partners

Sunshine Equity is a **fictional** growth-equity firm used for product demos. It partners with software and tech-enabled services companies that help industrial and infrastructure operators electrify, measure, and run climate-adjacent assets.

This repo is the firm’s **AI brain**: curated knowledge under `firm/` plus published evidence from the firm wiki (`neutron/`).

## What we do

- **Invest** growth capital—often structured preferred equity—alongside management teams scaling climate-infrastructure software.
- **Partner** post-investment via boards, portfolio support, and a small bench of operating advisors.
- **Source** thematically: outbound market work (including **sprints**), network, and banker/management relationships. See [GLOSSARY.md](./GLOSSARY.md).

There is no public site. This universe exists only inside this demo repo.

## Funds

Sunshine invests through successive fund vehicles. For day-to-day work, “Fund I–II” is enough.

| Vehicle | Role (high level) |
|--------|---------------------|
| **Fund I** | Earlier vintage; Kilnwise and a handful of realized / residual marks. |
| **Fund II** | Current-era vintage; HelioGrid, Tidalspan, Amberline, and newer deployments. |

**Performance detail** (invested capital, MOIC, IRR, deal-level marks) lives in confidential track-record and LP reporting under `../neutron/documents/financial-report/`—do not invent numbers; cite those sources.

**Co-investments** may sit alongside the main fund on specific deals; treat fund labeling on a company as deal-specific and check the latest IC/LP materials in neutron when precision matters.
